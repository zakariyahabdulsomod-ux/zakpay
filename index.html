Frequently asked questions

What is ZakPay?

ZakPay is a digital wallet for sending and receiving money, paying bills and buying airtime and data.

How do I create an account?

Select Get Started, enter your name, email and a password, then follow the prompts.

How can I send money?

Open Send Money, enter the recipient and amount, review the details and confirm.

Is ZakPay secure?

ZakPay uses secure sign-in, transaction confirmation, encrypted data and fraud monitoring to help protect your account.

Can I pay bills with ZakPay?

Yes. Choose Bills, pick a category, enter your account number and amount.
